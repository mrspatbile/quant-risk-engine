# ELTIF 2.0 -- European Long-Term Investment Fund

## Legislative Framework

| Instrument | Reference | Status | Content |
|------------|-----------|--------|---------|
| ELTIF 1.0 | Regulation (EU) 2015/760 | Replaced | Original framework -- largely unused due to restrictive rules |
| ELTIF 2.0 | Regulation (EU) 2023/606 | **Directly applicable** -- in force 10 January 2024 | Revised framework -- retail access, broader eligible assets, liquidity windows |
| ELTIF 2.0 RTS | Commission Delegated Regulation 2024/2759 | Directly applicable -- in force November 2024 | Redemption policy, liquidity management, cost disclosure |

**Directly applicable** -- no member state transposition. Luxembourg ELTIF 2.0
funds authorised by CSSF under the Regulation directly.

**Why ELTIF 1.0 failed:** fewer than 100 funds launched in 8 years. Minimum
investment of EUR 10,000 for retail, rigid eligible asset rules, no liquidity
provisions, and complex distribution rules made it commercially unviable.

**ELTIF 2.0 context:** part of the EU Capital Markets Union agenda -- channel
retail savings into long-term productive investment (infrastructure, private
equity, private credit, real estate). Luxembourg is the primary domicile.

---

## Scope and Structure

**ELTIF is an AIF** -- must be managed by a fully authorised AIFM under AIFMD.
CSSF authorises both the ELTIF product and the AIFM.

**Target investors:** professional and retail investors. Retail access is the
key policy objective of ELTIF 2.0 -- bringing private markets to mass affluent
and retail investors via wealth managers and private banks.

---

## 1. Eligible Assets

### Qualifying Portfolio Assets (must constitute at least 55% of capital)

| Asset type | Conditions |
|------------|-----------|
| Equity / quasi-equity in qualifying portfolio undertakings | Unlisted, or listed with market cap < EUR 1.5bn; established in EU or third country with AML/tax standards |
| Debt instruments in qualifying portfolio undertakings | Same issuer conditions as equity |
| Units in other ELTIFs, EuVECAs, EuSEFs | Eligible -- fund of funds structures allowed |
| Real assets | Direct investment -- minimum value EUR 1M; infrastructure, vessels, aircraft, IP, real estate |
| Green bonds | Issued under EU Green Bond Standard |

**Qualifying portfolio undertaking:** not a financial undertaking (no banks,
insurers, AIFMs unless infrastructure-focused); not listed above EUR 1.5bn
market cap; requires long-term investment.

### Liquid Assets (up to 45% of capital)

Standard UCITS-eligible liquid assets -- listed equities, bonds, money market
instruments. Used for liquidity management and to meet redemptions.

---

## 2. Diversification and Concentration

| Rule | Limit |
|------|-------|
| Single qualifying portfolio asset | Max 20% of capital |
| Single real asset | Max 20% of capital |
| Single UCITS / AIF | Max 20% of capital |
| Liquid assets (single issuer) | Max 10% of capital -- UCITS rules apply |
| Aggregate exposure to other ELTIFs / EuVECAs / EuSEFs | Max 20% of capital |
| Aggregate short positions | Max 10% of capital |

**Borrowing:** up to 50% of NAV for ELTIFs marketed to retail investors;
up to 100% of NAV for professional-only ELTIFs.

---

## 3. Liquidity -- The Key Innovation of ELTIF 2.0

ELTIF 1.0 was closed-ended with no liquidity. ELTIF 2.0 allows:

### Optional Liquidity Window

Open-ended or semi-open ELTIFs may offer periodic redemptions subject to:
- Redemption policy specified in fund rules
- Maximum redemption frequency and notice periods defined
- Liquidity management tools (LMTs) mandatory -- aligned with AIFMD II
- Liquid asset sleeve sized to meet expected redemptions
- Redemption requests matched against new subscriptions where possible

### Liquidity Management Tools (RTS 2024/2759)

ELTIFs with liquidity windows must have at minimum:
- Redemption gates
- Notice periods

And make available at least one anti-dilution tool:
- Swing pricing
- Anti-dilution levy

### Closed-Ended Structure

ELTIFs may remain closed-ended -- no redemption before end of life.
Life must be consistent with long-term nature of assets. Extension possible
with investor approval.

## 4. Retail Investor Access -- Key Changes from ELTIF 1.0

| Rule | ELTIF 1.0 | ELTIF 2.0 |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| Minimum investment | EUR 10,000 | **No minimum** |
| Appropriateness test | Required | Required -- MiFID suitability for advised sales |
| Portfolio diversification check | Required if portfolio < EUR 500k | **Removed** |
| Marketing to retail | Permitted with restrictions | Permitted -- KIID / PRIIPs KID required |
| Distribution | Direct and via intermediaries | Via MiFID distributors, wealth managers, private banks |

**No minimum investment** is the most significant change -- opens ELTIF to
mass affluent retail via wealth management platforms.

**Suitability:** MiFID II suitability assessment mandatory for retail investors
purchasing via advice. ELTIF distributor must assess client knowledge, experience,
financial situation, and investment objectives.

---

## 5. Costs and Disclosure

**PRIIPs KID mandatory** for retail ELTIFs -- standard three-page document
with SRI, performance scenarios, and cost breakdown.

**Cost transparency (RTS 2024/2759):**

| Cost category | Disclosure requirement |
|---------------|----------------------|
| Management fee | Annual, % of NAV |
| Performance fee | Methodology, hurdle rate, high watermark |
| Transaction costs | Estimated annual |
| Carried interest | Structure and calculation |
| Subscription / redemption fees | If applicable |
| Liquidity management costs | Swing factor or ADL methodology |

**Total cost ratio (TCR):** aggregate of all costs as % of NAV -- mandatory
disclosure in PRIIPs KID and annual report.

---

## 6. Luxembourg ELTIF Market

Luxembourg dominates EU ELTIF domiciliation -- approximately 60% of all
ELTIF 2.0 funds registered in Luxembourg as of early 2025.

**Why Luxembourg:**
- Established AIFMD infrastructure -- depositary network, administrators, legal expertise
- CSSF experience with AIF authorisation
- EU passport -- single authorisation, distribution across all EU member states
- Tax neutral at fund level (no withholding on distributions for eligible investors)
- RAIF structure not available for ELTIFs -- must be CSSF-authorised product

**CSSF ELTIF authorisation:** typically 2-3 months. CSSF has dedicated ELTIF
team following ELTIF 2.0 launch.

## 7. Key Metrics and KPIs

| Metric | Definition | Regulatory use |
|--------|-----------|---------------|
| Qualifying asset ratio | Qualifying portfolio assets / total capital | Min 55% -- ELTIF compliance |
| Illiquid asset ratio | Illiquid assets / total NAV | Liquidity management |
| Liquid sleeve | Liquid assets available for redemptions | Redemption coverage |
| Borrowing ratio | Total borrowings / NAV | Max 50% retail, 100% professional |
| Redemption coverage ratio | Liquid assets / redemption requests | LMT trigger calibration |
| TCR | Total cost ratio -- all costs / NAV | PRIIPs KID disclosure |
| Distribution reach | Number of EU member states with active distribution | Passport utilisation |

---

## 8. Interaction with Other Regulation

| Regulation | Interaction |
|------------|-------------|
| AIFMD / AIFMD II | ELTIF must be managed by authorised AIFM -- all AIFMD obligations apply including Annex IV reporting and LMTs |
| PRIIPs | PRIIPs KID mandatory for retail ELTIFs -- SRI, performance scenarios, cost disclosure |
| MiFID II | Suitability assessment mandatory for retail distribution via advice; product governance applies |
| EMIR | ELTIF using derivatives subject to EMIR clearing and reporting obligations |
| IFRS 13 | Level 3 fair value for illiquid portfolio assets -- appraised value, DCF |
| UCITS | ELTIF is not UCITS -- no UCITS passport; separate ELTIF EU passport under Regulation |
